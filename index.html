Good to know

    What is Second Saturday

    One night a month. The whole Church, together.

    A welcoming, joy-filled worship night where anyone, believer or not, can encounter the love of Jesus and find a place to belong in a local church.

    Rooted in the Great Commission, we go where people are, lower every barrier, and make it easy to take a next step toward faith and community. It's one night a month, and the goal is a lifetime of faith and friendship.

    “Go therefore and make disciples of all nations…” Matthew 28:19–20

    What to expect

    Come as you are

    No prior faith experience required. No pressure. Just a warm room, real worship, and people glad you came.

    Live worship

    A full band leads an evening of heartfelt, unhurried worship: believers from churches across our area, serving together.

    Fellowship

    Doors open early and we linger afterward. Come for the connection. There's always conversation and a friendly face.

    A next step

    Every night is a bridge to a church home. Wherever you are, we'll help you take the next step toward faith and a community that lasts.

    Our core values

    What we hold to

    Accessible

    Anyone can invite anyone. No prior faith experience required.

    Unifying

    Local churches serving together, not in competition.

    Rooted

    Bible-centered, Church-supported, and Christ-exalting in everything we do.

    Connective

    Every night is a bridge from a worship night to a church home.

    Our vision

    Churches of the 570, shoulder to shoulder

    We envision a Central Pennsylvania where local churches stand together, unified in the spirit of Jesus' prayer in John 17, reaching the lost, welcoming the searching, and connecting every person to a community where they can grow, belong, and thrive.

    What we believe

    Gathered around the historic essentials

    The great truths the Church has confessed together across centuries, denominations, and traditions: the shared foundation for all true Christian fellowship.

    The Apostles' Creed

    We believe in God the Father Almighty, maker of heaven and earth.

    We believe in Jesus Christ, His only Son, our Lord, who was conceived by the Holy Spirit and born of the Virgin Mary, who suffered under Pontius Pilate, was crucified, died, and was buried, who rose again on the third day from the dead, who ascended into heaven and is seated at the right hand of the Father, and who will come again to judge the living and the dead.

    We believe in the Holy Spirit, the holy universal Church, the communion of saints, the forgiveness of sins, the resurrection of the body, and the life everlasting. Amen.

    • The Bible is the inspired, inerrant, authoritative Word of God and the final rule for faith and life.
    • Salvation is by grace alone, through faith alone, in Christ alone.
    • Every believer is called to life in community with the local church.
    • Jesus is Lord over all: past, present, and future.
    A note on our unity

    Faithful, Bible-believing churches hold a range of convictions on secondary matters: modes of baptism, worship style, church governance, spiritual gifts, and more. Second Saturday is not the place to settle those differences; we celebrate them as evidence of a rich and diverse body of Christ. When questions arise, we point people back to Scripture and their home church. Our unity rests not on uniformity, but on the person and work of Jesus Christ, in whom all things hold together (Colossians 1:17).
