About Second Saturday

A monthly worship night shared by churches across Central PA.

Who we are

One night a month. The whole Church, together.

Second Saturday is a worship night for every church in our area. Each month a different church hosts, and believers from across Central PA lead worship together.

Our goal is simple: help people meet Jesus and find a church home. It’s one night a month, and we hope it leads to a lifetime of faith and friendship.

“Go therefore and make disciples of all nations…” Matthew 28:19

What to expect

Come as you are

No dress code and no pressure. You don’t need any church background to come.

Worship

A band made up of musicians from different local churches leads the night.

Fellowship

Come early and stay after. It’s a good chance to meet people from other churches.

A church home

Second Saturday doesn’t replace your church. If you don’t have one, we’d love to help you find one nearby.

Our values

What we hold to

Open

Anyone can come, and anyone can invite a friend.

United

Local churches working together, not competing.

Rooted

Centered on the Bible and on Jesus.

Connected

Every night points people toward a local church.

Our vision

The churches of the 570, together

We pray for a Central Pennsylvania where churches stand together, as Jesus prayed in John 17. Together we can reach people who don’t know Him, welcome people who are searching, and help everyone find a church where they can grow.
